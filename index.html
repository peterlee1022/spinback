Press your own record

  1. Open your Spotify privacy settings and scroll to Download your data.
  2. Tick Account data (about 5 days, last year of listening) or Extended streaming history (up to 30 days, every year you have ever streamed).
  3. When the email shows up, download the zip and drop it anywhere on this page. No need to unzip it.

Your files never leave this tab. Everything is parsed locally in your browser, nothing gets uploaded.
